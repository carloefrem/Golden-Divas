# Golden Divas - Lebanese Musical Heritage

A dynamic, two-team trivia website designed to celebrate, preserve, and teach the rich history of iconic Lebanese artists who shaped and influenced music across the Arab world. 

Users can test their knowledge on legendary figures such as **Fairouz, Sabah, Ziad Rahbani**, and many other cultural icons while competing in a fast-paced, interactive game environment.

---

## Game Rules & Mechanics

The game pits two teams (**Team A** and **Team B**) against each other in a battle of musical knowledge:

* **Primary Turn:** The active team has **30 seconds** to answer a music trivia question. A correct answer awards **2 points**.
* **Steal Opportunity:** If the active team answers incorrectly or runs out of time, the opposing team gets a chance to "steal." They are given a shortened **15-second** timer and earn **1 point** for a correct answer.
* **Winning Condition:** The team with the highest point total at the end of all rounds is declared the winner.

### Educational Insights
Beyond competition, this project focuses on cultural education. After every single question, the game displays a **detailed story or historical explanation** about the artist or song featured. This ensures players walk away with a deeper appreciation of the Lebanese musical heritage.

---

## Features

- **Interactive Timer:** Dynamic 30-second and 15-second countdown functionality for active turns and steals.
- **Score Tracking:** Real-time point accumulation and turn management for Team A and Team B.
- **Rich Educational Content:** Curated historical snippets and trivia surrounding legendary Arab music influencers.
- **Responsive Web Interface:** Built to be intuitive, clean, and accessible directly via a web browser.
